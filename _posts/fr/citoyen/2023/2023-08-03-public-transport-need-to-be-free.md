---
title: Public transport need to be free
translations:
    fr: de-la-gratuite-des-transports-publics
---

Public transport is a public service. Like roads, schools, hospitals, libraries, parks and gardens, etc.

Yet, they are considered a commercial activity. And this commercial activity is considered "in deficit" by public authorities. And so, we are told that users must pay more to "balance the accounts".

How to fill the deficit? By increasing fares, reducing services, privatizing, making users pay...

Meanwhile, the deficit only holds from this angle: if we completely stop charging users, there is no more commercial activity. There is no more deficit, just a public service.
